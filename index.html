Frequently asked questions

How do I pay?

Open the Payment page, choose a package and pay with M-Pesa using STK push, Paybill or Till. Your payment will show in your dashboard.

How fast do you respond?

We aim to reply to every message the same day. For urgent matters, call or WhatsApp us directly.

Can I get a refund?

Contact support within 48 hours of payment and we'll review your case fairly.

Is my information safe?

We only ask for what is needed to serve you and never share your details with third parties.
